Things I've built

Job Search Automation System

An end-to-end job-search pipeline that parses job-alert emails into a Google Sheet, scores role fit with an LLM, deduplicates listings, and tracks every application's status. Added 9 connectors that pull postings straight from company ATS boards and job APIs, cutting LinkedIn's share of intake from 99% to under half. Then audited the pipeline's own funnel data row by row: found marketing emails being logged as interviews and ~30% of rows as noise, and rebuilt the metrics around human-confirmed outcomes only. Closed postings are flagged automatically, and every strong match carries a one-click people search for referral contacts. Built with AI coding assistance (Claude Code).

Google Apps Script Google Sheets Gmail API OpenAI API
RM: a relationship manager that plays like a game

A personal tracker for the 100+ people in my life, built as a game: each person has a level, a warmth that fades with silence, a notes Codex and quests. I log a call by typing a sentence into Telegram or the app; an AI turns the words into structured facts, but fixed rules in code do all the scoring, so nothing a message says can award points. A daily job writes the quests and sends one short Telegram digest. Runs on Cloudflare Workers and D1 behind a private login, with 400+ automated tests. Built with AI coding assistance (Claude Code): my focus was the game rules, the product decisions and validating the output.

Photo Food Logger and kitchen coach

Photograph a meal and it is logged with calories and macros into a Google Sheet I own. It reads the plate, asks one question when a missing amount would change the number, tracks the pantry (what is in the kitchen and what is about to go off), plans the day's meals from what is there, and answers questions as a coach. Corrections teach it: it remembers the foods and portions I fix. A harness to compare AI models against meals with known calories is built, so the numbers can be checked before they are trusted. In daily use since September. Built with AI coding assistance (Claude Code).

Personal Finance Manager

Turns bank and card statements into one categorised ledger in Google Sheets. A deterministic importer reads six statement formats and refuses to write unless every statement's parsed total ties to the total printed on it, so a dropped or duplicated row cannot slip in. Merchants are categorised from a map learned from rows already in the sheet; new ones are flagged for review instead of guessed. Budget, month-picker and portfolio views sit on top. Built with AI coding assistance (Claude Code).
